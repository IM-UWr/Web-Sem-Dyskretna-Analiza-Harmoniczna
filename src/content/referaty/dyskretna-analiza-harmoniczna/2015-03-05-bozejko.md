We give an example of special generalized Gaussian process B_{p}, p\in (0,1), such that for p=1 we get classical Bose Gaussian field and for p=0 we obtain very interesting process related to Boolean Gaussian process. That process is related with function t_{p} on 2- partitions done as follows:
t_{p} (V) = p ^{n-s(V)},
where s(V) =card{ k < n+1: {k, 2n-k+1} is a block of 2-partition V in {1,2,...,2n}}. That will be also presented connections with generalized Laguerre polynomials. (The paper with Wojciech Bożejko and Karol Penson.)
