Haagerup property and Kazhdan's Property (T), both in the
classical and quantum setting, can be viewed as strong negations of each
other, related to the representation theory of a given (quantum) group. I
will discuss these two concepts, focusing on the approach via `typical'
representations. The talk will be based on the work in progress with M.Daws
and S.White.
