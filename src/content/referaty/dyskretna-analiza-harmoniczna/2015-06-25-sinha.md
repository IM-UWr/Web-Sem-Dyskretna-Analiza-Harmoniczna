One has the Krein's trace formula (1960's) for the trace of the difference
of a function of two self-adjoint operators, which differ by a trace-class
operator, and then one has the more recent (late 1970-1980)
Helton-Howe-Carey-Pincus formula for the trace of the commutator between
a function of a pure hyponormal operator T and another of T*-- both are
quite beautiful formulae. Here a proof will be given to show that the second
one (in its Cartesian form) is a consequence of the first.
