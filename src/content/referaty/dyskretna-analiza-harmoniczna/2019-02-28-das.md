In the first part of this talk, we will briefly review the theory of Hudson-Parthasarathy (HP) stochastic calculus and stochastic dilation of Markov semigroups in general. In the second half, we will consider CQG algebras and we will
show that any translation invariant Markov semigroup on a CQG algebra can be stochastically dilated using Hudson-Parthasarahty calculus. Based on a joint work with Martin Lindsay.
