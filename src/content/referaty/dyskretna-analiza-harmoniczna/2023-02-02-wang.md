I will present a complete answer to the question of factoriality of the q-deformed Araki-Woods von Neumann algebras in most cases. In particular, the q-deformed Araki-Woods von Neumann algebras are factors for all q ∈ (−1, 1) whenever the dimension of the underlying real Hilbert space is greater than 3. In the 2-dimensional case they are factors as well for all q so long as the deformation is sufficiently large. Our new approach essentially takes advantage of the type III deformation, which does NOT apply to the tracial setting. The talk is based on joint work with Panchugopal Bikram, Kunal Mukherjee and Eric Ricard.
