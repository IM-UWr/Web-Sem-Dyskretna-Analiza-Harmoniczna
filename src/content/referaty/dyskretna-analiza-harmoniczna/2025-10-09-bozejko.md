In my talk, I will consider the results on projections that are free and conditionally free. Following Voiculescu, Arizmendi, and Vargas, we get some more general results on Tsallis entropy functions using the formula:
$$T_q(x,y) = {\rm max}\left\{\left(x^{1-q} + y^{1-q} -1\right)^{\frac{1}{1-q}},0\right\} .$$
