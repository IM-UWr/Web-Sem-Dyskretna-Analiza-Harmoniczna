The Bercovici–Pata bijection relates infinitely divisible distributions with respect to classical, free and Boolean convolutions and is conveniently expressed in terms of the corresponding cumulants. We present an operator-valued version of the Boolean-to-free part of this bijection, formulated via $B$-valued Boolean and free cumulants and their relation through non-crossing and interval partitions. As an application we obtain new formulas for operator-valued free convolution powers $\mu^{\boxplus t}$ for $t \ge 1$ and describe when these powers are again Boolean infinitely divisible.
